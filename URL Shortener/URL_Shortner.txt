Functional Requirement:
-----------------------
- 100M new short URLs per month
- A 100-to-1-ratio between redirects and URL creation
- Links are permanent by default, with optional expiration
- Users may optionally choose a custom alias
- Basic asynchronous click analytics are required
- Redirect availability and latency are more important than immediately consistent analytics
- The service operates across multiple regions

Non-Functional Requirement:
---------------------------
- Low latency
- High availability
- Massive scale
- (And so on...)

Assumptions:
------------
- 100M new short URLs creation per month
-- 100M / 30 / 86400 sec = 39 creations per seconds
-- Peak: 10x: 390 creations per seconds

- Redirect traffic: 100 redirects for every single creation
-- 39 * 100 = 3900 redirects per second on average
-- Peak: 10x: 390000 redirects per second

- Storage: 1 URL record consumes approx 1KB after including the URL, metadata, indexes, indexes, and database overhead
-- 100M records times 1KB = 100 GB per month
-- 100GB * 12 = 1.2 TB per year storage requirement

API Design:
-----------
POST /v1/urls
GET /{shortCode}

DB Schema:
----------
- Distributed key-value or wide-column DB: DynamoDB or Cassandra: (Table: short_code(PK), long_Url, created_at, expires_at, status, owner_id, redirect_type, version, url_hash)
